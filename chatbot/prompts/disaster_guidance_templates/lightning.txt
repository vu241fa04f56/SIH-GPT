LIGHTNING SAFETY — OFFICIAL GUIDANCE
Source: India Meteorological Department (IMD) & NDMA Damini Alert System

WHEN THUNDER IS HEARD (30-30 Rule: seek shelter if lightning-thunder gap < 30 seconds):
1. Go indoors immediately — a substantial building or a hard-topped vehicle.
2. Avoid open structures (bus shelters, thatched huts, rain shelters).
3. Stay indoors for at least 30 minutes after the last thunder.

INDOORS:
- Avoid contact with corded phones, electrical equipment, plumbing.
- Stay away from windows, doors, porches, and concrete floors/walls (they can conduct current).
- Unplug electronic equipment before the storm arrives.

IF CAUGHT OUTDOORS — NO SAFE SHELTER NEARBY:
1. Move away from open fields, hilltops, ridge tops.
2. Never shelter under isolated tall trees, cliff overhangs, or near bodies of water.
3. If in a forest, shelter near lower trees.
4. Move away from metal objects (fences, machinery, motorcycles).
5. If you feel your hair stand on end (lightning imminent): Crouch down on balls of feet, feet together, head down, hands over ears. Do NOT lie flat.

BOATS AND WATER:
- Get off water immediately. Lightning can strike a kilometre away from the storm.

IF SOMEONE IS STRUCK:
- Call 108 immediately. Lightning strike victims do NOT carry electrical charge — it is safe to help them.
- Begin CPR if no pulse.

HELPLINE: Ambulance 108 | Damini Lightning Alert App (free, IMD)
