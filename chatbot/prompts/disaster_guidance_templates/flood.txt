FLOOD EARLY WARNING — OFFICIAL GUIDANCE
Source: National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), India

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Move to higher ground immediately. Do not wait for official evacuation order.
2. Avoid walking or driving through floodwaters. 15 cm of moving water can knock a person down; 60 cm can sweep away a vehicle.
3. If trapped in a building, move to the highest floor. Do NOT enter the attic (risk of being trapped if water keeps rising). Signal for rescue from windows or rooftop.
4. Disconnect electrical appliances. Do not touch electrical equipment if wet or standing in water.
5. Avoid contact with floodwater — it may be contaminated with sewage, chemicals, or debris.

PREPARATION:
- Keep emergency documents (Aadhaar, insurance) in a waterproof bag.
- Store drinking water in sealed containers (supply may be disrupted).
- Keep a battery-powered or hand-crank radio for official updates.

AFTER THE FLOOD:
- Do not return home until authorities declare it safe.
- Clean and disinfect all surfaces that contacted floodwater.
- Watch for damaged infrastructure (roads, bridges, electrical lines).
- Seek medical attention if you have open wounds that contacted floodwater.

HELPLINE: NDMA 1078 | State Disaster Helpline 1070 | Police 100 | Ambulance 108
