CYCLONE EARLY WARNING — OFFICIAL GUIDANCE
Source: India Meteorological Department (IMD) & NDMA

BEFORE THE CYCLONE (if 48–72 hrs warning):
1. Evacuate if you live in a low-lying coastal area. Follow district administration instructions.
2. Secure or bring inside loose objects (furniture, vehicles, gas cylinders).
3. Board up windows or use storm shutters. Reinforce doors.
4. Stock up: 3-day supply of water, food, medicines, torch, batteries, cash.
5. Keep mobile phones fully charged. Download the Sachet/Damini app for alerts.

DURING THE CYCLONE:
1. Stay indoors. Go to the strongest part of your building (internal wall, lowest floor if not flood-prone).
2. Keep away from windows and glass doors.
3. Do NOT go outside during the eye of the storm — it will resume.
4. If your building is at risk, move to designated cyclone shelters.

AFTER THE CYCLONE:
1. Do not enter damaged buildings until inspected.
2. Beware of fallen power lines, debris, and waterlogged roads.
3. Use only stored/boiled water — supply may be contaminated.
4. Report injured/missing persons to the nearest relief camp.
