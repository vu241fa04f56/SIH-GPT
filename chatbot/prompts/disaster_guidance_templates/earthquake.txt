EARTHQUAKE EARLY WARNING — OFFICIAL GUIDANCE
Source: National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), India

DURING SHAKING — DROP, COVER, HOLD ON:
1. DROP to your hands and knees immediately.
2. COVER your head and neck with your arms. Shelter under a sturdy desk/table if nearby; if not, move to an inside wall away from windows.
3. HOLD ON until shaking stops. Do NOT run outside during shaking.
4. If in bed: Stay there, protect your head with a pillow.
5. If outdoors: Move away from buildings, trees, streetlights, and utility wires. Drop to the ground and stay there.
6. If in a vehicle: Pull over away from buildings/overpasses. Stay inside with seatbelt on until shaking stops.

AFTER SHAKING STOPS:
1. Expect aftershocks. Each time you feel one, Drop-Cover-Hold.
2. Check yourself and others for injuries. Do not move seriously injured people.
3. Inspect your home for structural damage. If unsafe, evacuate.
4. Do not use elevators.
5. Check for gas leaks (smell, hissing). If suspected: open windows, leave building, call fire department from outside.
6. Stay away from damaged areas. Watch for falling debris.
